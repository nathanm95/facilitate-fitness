## Short description (`--description`)

Your day's fitness at a glance: steps against your goal, heart rate, calories, distance, active minutes and sleep.

## Full description

Facilitate Fitness is back, rebuilt from scratch for the Pebble Time 2 and
Pebble Round 2. Same lavender, same pink badge with the diamond and the
runner, now with everything Pebble Health knows about your day.

- Steps against your daily goal on a ring: one lap is your goal, and a
  second lap starts when you beat it
- A small tick on the ring shows where you'd usually be by now, so you can
  see at a glance if you're ahead or behind your normal day
- Heart rate on the Pebble Time 2 (the Round 2 has no heart-rate sensor)
- Active calories, distance walked, active minutes and last night's sleep
- Date and battery
- Three themes: Classic lavender, Midnight black and Blush pink
- Step goal of 5,000 to 20,000, or "my usual day" from Pebble Health
- Distance in kilometres or miles, following your Health setting by default
- Updates once a minute, so it's easy on the battery

Turn on Pebble Health in the Pebble app. For heart rate, also turn on
heart-rate monitoring there. The face never switches the sensor on itself,
so it costs no extra battery.

For Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.
